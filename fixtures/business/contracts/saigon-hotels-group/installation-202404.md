## Parties and Type

This installation contract is entered into between Hikari Home (the "Supplier") and Saigon Hotels Group (the "Customer"). Under it, the Supplier will deliver and install Hikari Home appliances, including air conditioners and water heaters, in the Customer's hotel properties in Vietnam. The Sales department prepared the contract on 22 March 2024.

## Term and Value

The contract is effective from 10 April 2024 and expires on 9 April 2027. The total contract value is 243,000,000 VND, which covers equipment installation, commissioning, testing, and handover documentation for each property.

## Payment Terms

The Supplier will issue invoices upon completion of each installation milestone. The Customer shall pay each invoice within 60 days of receipt, by bank transfer in Vietnamese dong.

## Renewal

If either party wishes to renew or renegotiate the contract, it must notify the other party in writing at least 60 days before the expiry date of 9 April 2027. Without such notice, the contract ends on that date and any further installation work will require a new agreement.
