This Installation Contract is made between Hikari Home (the "Contractor") and Saigon Hotels Group (the "Customer") in Vietnam. The document was prepared by the Sales department and updated on 2025-03-31.

## Term and Value

The contract is effective from 2025-04-20 to 2026-04-19. The total contract value is 4,252,000,000 VND, covering equipment, installation labor, testing and commissioning at each site.

## Payment Terms

The Customer shall settle each invoice within 60 days of the invoice date by bank transfer. Invoices are issued upon completion and acceptance of each installation batch.

## Renewal
