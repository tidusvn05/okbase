## Parties and Type

This maintenance contract is made between Hikari Home (the "Provider") and Tokyo Denki Retail (the "Customer") for the servicing of Hikari Home appliances across the Customer's retail stores in Japan. It was prepared by the Sales department and finalized on 15 December 2025.

## Term and Value

The contract takes effect on 27 December 2025 and remains in force until 26 December 2028, a period of three years. The total contract value is 46,950,000 JPY, covering scheduled inspections, repairs, replacement parts, and on-site technician visits for the appliances listed in the service schedule.

## Payment Terms

The Provider will invoice the Customer in accordance with the agreed billing schedule. Each invoice is payable within 30 days of the invoice date, by bank transfer to the account designated by the Provider. Late payments may be subject to interest as set out in the general terms.

## Renewal

Either party wishing to renew, amend, or terminate the contract at the end of the term must give written notice at least 30 days before the expiry date of 26 December 2028. If no notice is given, the parties will meet to discuss renewal conditions before the contract lapses.
