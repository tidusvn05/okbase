## Parties and Scope

This Installation Contract is concluded between Hikari Home (the "Contractor") and Osaka Office Supply (the "Customer"), based in Japan. Hikari Home will install appliances purchased by the Customer at its offices and facilities, including delivery to site, mounting, wiring and operational checks. The Sales department prepared this contract, last updated on 2025-05-14.

## Term and Value

The contract takes effect on 2025-05-22 and continues until 2027-05-21. The total contract value is 6,910,000 JPY, which covers labor, standard installation materials and post-installation testing.

## Payment Terms

The Customer shall pay each invoice within 30 days of the invoice date, by bank transfer to the account specified by Hikari Home.

## Renewal

A party that wishes to renew, modify or end the contract must notify the other in writing at least 60 days before the end of the term. If no notice is given, the parties will meet to agree on terms for any continued installation work.
