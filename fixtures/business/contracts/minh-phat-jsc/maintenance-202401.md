## Parties and Contract Type

This maintenance contract is entered into between Hikari Home (the "Provider") and Minh Phat JSC (the "Customer"). The Provider will deliver scheduled preventive maintenance, on-site repairs and spare-part replacement for home appliances installed at the Customer's facilities in Vietnam.

## Term and Value

The contract is effective from January 27, 2024 to January 26, 2026, a term of two years. The total contract value is 4,086,000,000 VND, covering labor, standard spare parts and response-time commitments.

## Payment Terms

The Provider will invoice the Customer quarterly. The Customer shall pay each invoice by bank transfer within 60 days of receipt. Late payments may be subject to interest as agreed in writing.

## Renewal

Either party wishing to renew, renegotiate or terminate the contract must give written notice at least 90 days before the expiry date. Absent such notice, the parties will meet to discuss renewal terms.
