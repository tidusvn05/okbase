## Parties and Scope

This Supply Contract is entered into between Hikari Home (the "Supplier") and Sakura Trading (the "Customer"), a wholesale buyer in Japan. Hikari Home agrees to supply home appliances from its Japan catalogue according to purchase orders issued by the Customer during the term. The contract was prepared by the Sales department and last updated on 2025-10-18.

## Term and Value

The contract takes effect on 2025-10-31 and remains in force until 2027-10-27. The total contract value is 28,940,000 JPY, covering all product deliveries made under the agreement. Individual orders will specify models, quantities and delivery dates, and are invoiced against this overall value.

## Payment Terms

The Customer shall pay each invoice within 45 days of the invoice date, by bank transfer to the account designated by Hikari Home. Late payments may be subject to interest as set out in the general terms and conditions.

## Renewal and Termination

Either party wishing to renew, renegotiate or end the agreement must give written notice at least 60 days before the end of the term. If no notice is given, the parties will meet to discuss continuation on updated terms. Termination for material breach is governed by the general terms.
