## Parties and Contract Type
This distribution agreement is made between Hikari Home (the Supplier) and Sakura Trading (the Distributor) for the Japanese market. The Distributor is authorized to purchase and resell Hikari Home home appliances within the agreed territory.

## Term
The agreement is effective from 2024-10-10 and remains in force until 2027-10-09, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its provisions.

## Contract Value and Payment
The total contract value is 6,770,000 JPY. The Distributor shall pay each invoice within 30 days of receipt by bank transfer to the account designated by the Supplier. Late payments may be subject to interest as set out in the agreement.

## Renewal
Either party wishing to renew the agreement must give written notice at least 90 days before the expiry date. Absent such notice, the agreement expires at the end of the term. Any amendment must be made in writing and signed by both parties.
