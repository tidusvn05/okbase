## Purpose and Scope

This SOP defines how employees and contractors at Hikari Home in Vietnam and Japan request access to internal systems, shared folders and applications. It has been effective since 1 February 2026 and applies to all departments.

## Request Process

1. The requester submits a ticket in the IT service portal, stating the system, the access level and the business justification.
2. The direct manager reviews the request and confirms that it is needed for the person's role.
3. The system owner approves or rejects the request.
4. IT provisions the access and notifies the requester.

Access follows the principle of least privilege. Administrator rights and access to finance or customer personal data require an additional approval from the IT Security lead.

## Service Levels and Review

Approvers must respond within 24 hours of receiving a request during business days. Requests not handled in that window are escalated automatically to the approver's manager. Urgent requests may be flagged in the ticket and handled by the on-call IT engineer.

All granted access is reviewed every quarter, and temporary access expires automatically at the end date. When an employee changes role or leaves the company, the manager must submit a removal ticket on the same day.
