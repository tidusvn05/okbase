## Purpose and Scope

This SOP covers the issuing, use, maintenance and replacement of company laptops for employees of Hikari Home in Vietnam and Japan. It has been effective since 21 October 2025.

## Issuing and Security Requirements

- Each laptop is registered in the asset inventory and assigned to one named user.
- Disk encryption, endpoint protection and the company management agent must be enabled before handover.
- Users must not install unlicensed software or disable security tools.
- Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to IT immediately so the device can be locked and wiped remotely.

## Maintenance and Replacement

Laptops are scheduled for replacement every 3 years from the date of issue. Devices with serious hardware faults may be replaced earlier, subject to approval from the IT manager. IT contacts the user about two months before the replacement date to arrange the exchange.

When returning a laptop, the user must back up work files to the approved cloud storage and hand the device to IT. IT wipes the data securely and either reassigns the device or disposes of it through a certified recycler, keeping a record of the process.
