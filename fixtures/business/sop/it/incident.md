## Purpose and Scope

This SOP describes how Hikari Home staff in Vietnam and Japan must report and respond to IT security incidents, such as phishing, malware, lost devices or suspected data leaks. It has been effective since 10 February 2026.

## Reporting

Any employee who notices or suspects an incident must report it within 30 minutes of discovery. Reports can be made through the IT Helpdesk hotline, the incident channel or by email to the security team. Include what happened, the affected system or device and the time it was noticed.

Do not attempt to investigate on your own, delete evidence or restart affected machines unless told to by IT.

## Response Steps

1. Triage: the IT on-call engineer classifies severity from Low to Critical.
2. Containment: affected accounts are disabled and devices are isolated from the network.
3. Eradication and recovery: the cause is removed and services are restored from clean backups.
4. Notification: for incidents involving personal data, Legal decides whether regulators and customers must be informed under Vietnamese and Japanese law.

## Follow-up

A post-incident review is held within five business days, and corrective actions are tracked until completion.
