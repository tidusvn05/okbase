## Purpose
This SOP describes how the Customer Support team handles warranty claims for Hikari Home appliances sold in Vietnam and Japan. It has been in effect since 28 April 2024.

## Eligibility Check
The agent first confirms the purchase date, model code, and serial number against the sales record. A claim is eligible when the product is within its warranty period and the fault is not caused by misuse, unauthorized repair, or accidental damage. Customers without a receipt may be verified through their registered account or the retailer's records.

## Required Evidence
To file a claim, customers must submit 2 photos: one showing the serial number label and one clearly showing the defect or damage. Claims lacking these photos are placed on hold, and the agent requests the missing items through the customer's preferred channel. Additional video may be requested for intermittent faults.

## Processing
Once approved, the agent arranges repair, replacement, or pickup by an authorized service partner and shares the tracking details with the customer. All decisions and evidence are attached to the ticket for audit and quality review.
