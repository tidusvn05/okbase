## Purpose
This SOP defines how the Customer Support team receives and resolves complaints about Hikari Home products and services in Vietnam and Japan. It was last updated on 23 November 2024 and remains the current version.

## Intake and Acknowledgement
Every complaint must be logged as a ticket tagged "complaint", regardless of the channel it arrives through. The agent acknowledges receipt to the customer the same business day, apologizes for the inconvenience, and explains the next steps. Complaints involving safety, injury, or legal claims must be reported to the CS manager immediately.

## Investigation and Resolution
The assigned agent gathers facts, reviews order and service history, and consults the relevant product or logistics team. The target is to resolve each complaint within 5 days of receipt. If more time is needed, the agent informs the customer of the delay and the reason, and updates the ticket accordingly. Proposed remedies, such as repair, replacement, or refund, follow the applicable approval limits.

## Closure
Before closing, confirm the outcome with the customer and record the root cause. Complaint trends are reviewed monthly to drive corrective actions.
