## Overview

The Legal department is announcing the upcoming office relocation and its effect on our regular work. This notice was issued on 3 June 2026. To keep contracts, corporate records, and compliance documents safe during the move, we ask all employees in Vietnam and Japan to follow the guidance below.

## Impact on Legal Services

- Contract review requests may take slightly longer while files are being transferred
- Original signed contracts and corporate seals will be moved only by Legal staff
- Notarization and government filing requests should be submitted early to avoid delays
- Physical archives will be temporarily unavailable during the move

## What We Need From You

Please submit any pending contract review requests before the relocation begins, and clearly mark urgent items. Do not leave signed agreements, NDAs, or other confidential papers on desks; deliver them to the Legal department or place them in the locked collection boxes provided.

After the move, new storage locations and contact details will be posted on the intranet. If you have questions about how the relocation affects a specific contract or filing, please contact the Legal department directly. Thank you for your cooperation.
