## Overview

The IT department will relocate to a new floor as part of the company's workspace consolidation. The new space offers a dedicated server-support room, improved network cabling and additional meeting areas. Facilities will share the exact move date and layout through the intranet.

## Impact on IT services

On the day of the move, the help desk will operate in a reduced mode. Please submit non-urgent requests through the ticketing portal ahead of time, and expect longer response times for the first day at the new location. Critical outages will still be handled by the on-call engineer. Planned maintenance windows for internal systems will be scheduled away from the move to avoid overlapping risks.

## What staff need to do

- Back up local files to the company cloud storage before the move.
- Label all monitors, docking stations and peripherals with your name.
- Do not disconnect or pack devices issued to other teams.
- Report any missing or damaged equipment to the help desk right after setup.

Office access cards for the new floor will be activated on the first working day. If your card does not work, contact the front desk or the IT help desk. Thank you for your cooperation and patience.
