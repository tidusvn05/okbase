## Coverage basics

**Q. What does the standard warranty cover?**

A. Hikari Home warrants that each appliance will be free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use. If a covered defect appears during the warranty period, we will repair or replace the product at no charge.

**Q. When does the warranty start?**

A. The warranty period begins on the date of purchase shown on your receipt or registered order. Please keep proof of purchase, as we need it to verify eligibility. Registering your product in the customer portal makes future claims faster.

## Filing a claim

**Q. How do I request warranty service?**

A. Contact Customer Support by phone, email or the web form with your model code, serial number, purchase date and a short description of the problem. Photos or a short video help us diagnose faster. An agent will confirm eligibility and arrange either a technician visit or a pickup, depending on the product and your location in Vietnam or Japan.

## Exclusions

**Q. What is not covered?**

A. Damage from misuse, unauthorized repair, power surges, liquids entering non-waterproof parts, or normal wear on consumables such as filters and seals is excluded. Commercial use is also outside the standard warranty. If we determine a claim is not covered, we will explain why and can offer a paid repair quote.
