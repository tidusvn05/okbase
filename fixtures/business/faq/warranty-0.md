## Coverage

**What does the standard warranty cover?**
Hikari Home warranty covers manufacturing defects and component failures that occur under normal household use. It does not cover damage caused by misuse, unauthorized repairs, power surges, or accidents.

**How long does the warranty last?**
The warranty period depends on the product category and is printed on the warranty card supplied in the box. Compressors and motors typically carry a longer period than other parts.

## Making a Claim

**How do I submit a claim?**
Contact Customer Support by phone or through the website, and provide the model code, serial number and proof of purchase. An agent will register the case and arrange a technician visit or a drop-off at an authorized service center.

**Do I need the original receipt?**
Yes. A receipt or order confirmation showing the purchase date is required. If you registered the product online, we can look up the purchase for you.

## Good to Know

Repairs performed under warranty do not extend the original warranty period. Replaced parts are covered for the remainder of the original term. For anything not answered here, please contact the CS team, who will be glad to help.
