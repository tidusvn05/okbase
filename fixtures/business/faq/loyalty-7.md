## Earning points

**Q. How do I earn loyalty points?**

A. Members earn points on eligible purchases made through Hikari Home stores and the official online shop. Points are credited after the return window for the order has closed. Accessories and extended-care plans also earn points, while gift cards and taxes do not.

**Q. Do I need an account to join?**

A. Yes. Sign up with your email or phone number in the customer portal. Existing customers can link earlier purchases by entering their order numbers, and our support team can help if an order does not appear.

## Tiers and benefits

**Q. What are the membership tiers?**

A. There are three tiers based on the amount you spend in a calendar year. Higher tiers receive a larger points rate, early access to seasonal sales and priority handling from Customer Support. Your tier is reviewed once a year, and you will receive an email before any change takes effect.

## Redeeming and expiry

**Q. How do I use my points?**

A. At checkout, choose to apply points to reduce the payment. Points cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred between accounts.

**Q. Do points expire?**

A. Yes. Points expire after a period of account inactivity. We send a reminder email before expiry so you have time to redeem them. Points from returned orders are removed automatically.
