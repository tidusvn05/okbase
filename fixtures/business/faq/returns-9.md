## Return eligibility

**Q. Can I return a product I bought?**

A. Yes, unused products in their original packaging with all accessories can be returned within the return window stated on your order confirmation. Items showing signs of use, damage caused by the customer, or missing parts may be declined or refunded only in part.

**Q. Are any products non-returnable?**

A. Installed built-in appliances, items customized to order and consumables such as filters that have been opened cannot be returned unless they are defective. Clearance items are marked at the time of sale.

## How to return

**Q. What is the process?**

A. Contact Customer Support with your order number and the reason for the return. We will issue a return authorization and arrange a pickup or provide a drop-off location in Vietnam or Japan. Please pack the item securely and include the authorization number. Do not ship without it, as we may not be able to match your parcel.

## Refunds

**Q. When will I get my refund?**

A. After the returned item passes inspection, refunds are sent to the original payment method. Card refunds may take a few billing cycles to show on your statement. If the return is due to a defect or our error, we cover the return shipping cost. Otherwise the shipping fee may be deducted.
