## Joining the Program

**Who can join?**
Any customer with a Hikari Home account can join the loyalty program at no cost. Enrollment happens automatically when you register a purchase, or you can sign up on our website or through a Customer Support agent.

**Is the program available in both countries?**
Yes. The program operates in Vietnam and Japan, and points are tracked per account in the local market.

## Earning and Using Points

**How do I earn points?**
You earn points on eligible purchases of appliances, accessories and extended service plans. Promotional campaigns may offer bonus points, which are announced on the website.

**How can I redeem points?**
Points can be applied as a discount at checkout or exchanged for selected accessories and service vouchers. Points cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Account Questions

**Do points expire?**
Points remain valid for a limited period shown in your account dashboard, and we send a reminder before they expire.

**What happens if I return a product?**
Points earned on a returned item are deducted from your balance. For other questions, please contact the CS team.
