## Overview

The HR department held its hiring review on 2026-06-30. Attendees were the HR director, recruiters, and hiring managers from Sales, Warehouse and Customer Support.

## Discussion

The team reviewed open positions in Vietnam and Japan. Demand is highest for warehouse operators, after-sales technicians and sales representatives ahead of the second-half product launches. Recruiters reported that technician roles take the longest to fill, averaging more than two months. Candidate quality from university partnerships was strong, and the group discussed expanding referral incentives to speed up placements. Onboarding capacity was also reviewed, as trainers are stretched during peak intake periods.

## Decisions

- The hiring and onboarding budget is fixed at 660 million VND for the coming period, covering job advertising, agency fees and training.
- Priority will go to after-sales technician and warehouse operator positions.
- HR will launch an updated referral program within the month.
- The next meeting will take place in 4 weeks to review hiring progress and spending.

Hiring managers will submit final headcount justifications to HR by the end of next week.
