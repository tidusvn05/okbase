## Overview

Date: 2026-02-21. Attendees: Sales department (regional managers for Vietnam and Japan, sales operations and the channel team). The meeting reviewed progress against the first-quarter KPIs for both markets.

## Discussion

- Vietnam retail sell-through is on track, driven by air purifiers and rice cookers, while online conversion is still below the target set in January.
- Japan showed steady repeat orders from existing distributors, but new-account acquisition lagged behind plan.
- The team agreed that the current KPI dashboard mixes gross and net revenue, which makes comparisons between regions unreliable. Sales operations will separate the two measures.
- Several managers asked for clearer targets on lead follow-up time and average deal size.

## Decisions

- The Sales KPI improvement budget for the next period is set at 270 million VND, to be used for training, dashboard rework and regional promotions.
- Sales operations will publish a revised KPI definition sheet before the next session.
- Each regional manager will submit an action plan for underperforming metrics.
- The next meeting will be held in 1 week to confirm the revised definitions and review the first action plans.
