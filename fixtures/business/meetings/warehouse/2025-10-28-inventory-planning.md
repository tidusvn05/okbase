## Overview

The Warehouse department met on 2025-10-28 to review inventory levels ahead of the year-end sales season. Attendees included the warehouse manager, the inventory control team, and representatives from logistics.

## Discussion

The team reviewed current stock of refrigerators, rice cookers and air purifiers across the Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City distribution centers. Fast-moving items are running lower than forecast, while some older washing machine models are occupying too much shelf space. Logistics raised concerns about supplier lead times, which have grown by several days since September. The group agreed that safety stock thresholds need to be recalculated using the last six months of sales data.

## Decisions

- The inventory replenishment and storage budget is set at 130 million VND, to be allocated mainly to fast-moving product lines and temporary racking.
- Inventory control will publish revised safety stock thresholds before the next review.
- Slow-moving stock will be flagged for discount consideration with the Sales team.
- The next meeting will be held in 4 weeks to check progress against these actions.

Action owners were assigned during the meeting and will report status by email in between sessions.
