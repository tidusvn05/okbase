## Scope

This policy covers all Hikari Home air purifiers sold through authorized channels in Japan. Version 2 takes effect on March 12, 2026 and applies to all purchases made on or after that date.

## Warranty Period

The standard warranty period is 12 months from the date of delivery, as shown on the purchase receipt or the electronic warranty record. During this period, Hikari Home repairs or replaces defective parts free of charge when the defect is attributable to manufacturing.

## Exclusions

- Damage caused by drops, liquid ingress, or unauthorized disassembly.
- Use of non-genuine filters or accessories.
- Consumable parts such as filters, which are not covered by the warranty.

## Claim Process

Customers should contact the Customer Support team with their proof of purchase and a description of the issue. Support will arrange a pickup or direct the customer to an authorized service center. Repairs are normally completed within ten business days, and the customer is notified of the outcome by email or phone.
