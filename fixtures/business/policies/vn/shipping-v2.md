## Scope and effective date

This policy governs shipping for Hikari Home online and phone orders delivered within Vietnam. It takes effect on 26 April 2026 and replaces the earlier shipping terms used by the Customer Support (CS) team.

## Free shipping

Orders with a merchandise total of 300,000 VND or more qualify for free standard shipping. Orders below this amount are charged the flat regional rate shown at checkout. The threshold is calculated after discounts and before any applicable taxes, and it excludes gift cards.

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 4 business days from the date the order is confirmed and packed. Remote areas may require additional time, and customers are notified when a delay is expected. Tracking numbers are sent by SMS and email once the parcel is handed to the carrier.

## Support handling

CS agents should check the order value against the free shipping threshold before quoting any shipping fee. If a parcel is not delivered within the standard window, agents open a carrier inquiry and update the customer within one business day.
