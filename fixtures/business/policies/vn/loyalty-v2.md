## Purpose and effective date

This policy sets out how loyalty points work for Hikari Home customers in Vietnam. It takes effect on 3 March 2026 and supersedes Loyalty Program Policy (Vietnam) v1 (policies/vn/loyalty-v1).

## Points expiry

Loyalty points now expire 6 months after the date they are issued, replacing the previous 24-month period. The shorter window encourages timely redemption and reduces the long-term point liability on our books. Customers can see their balance and expiry dates in the customer app.

## Customer communication

- Customer Support sends a reminder notification before points are due to expire.
- Expired points cannot be reinstated, except where a system error is confirmed by CS.
- Frontline staff should explain the change clearly to customers who joined under v1.

## Ownership and review

The CS department owns this policy and reviews redemption data each quarter. Any change proposals must be submitted to the marketing and finance teams for approval before being published.
