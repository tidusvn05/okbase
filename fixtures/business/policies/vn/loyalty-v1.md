## Status

This policy is deprecated. It was superseded by Loyalty Program Policy (Vietnam) v2 (policies/vn/loyalty-v2), which took effect on 3 March 2026. It is kept here for reference only and should not be used to answer current customer questions.

## Scope and effective date

Version 1 took effect on 12 June 2024 and applied to all Hikari Home customers in Vietnam who enrolled in the loyalty program through our stores, website or hotline.

## Points expiry

Under v1, earned loyalty points expired 24 months after the date they were issued. Customers could check their balance and upcoming expiry dates through the customer app or by contacting Customer Support. Expired points could not be reinstated.

## Handling of legacy cases

- Points issued while v1 was in force are handled according to the transition rules in v2.
- Customer Support should refer to the current policy for any request made after 3 March 2026.
- Questions about historical cases can be escalated to the CS team lead.
