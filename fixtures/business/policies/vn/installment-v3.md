## Status

This is a draft proposal for version 3 of the Vietnam installment policy and is pending approval. If approved, it would take effect on November 1, 2026 and supersede v2 (policies/vn/installment-v2). Until then, v2 remains in force.

## Proposed Eligibility

The draft raises the minimum order value for installment payment to 3,000,000 VND, compared with 2,000,000 VND under v2. The aim is to focus installment plans on higher-value appliances and reduce the administrative cost of small plans.

## Proposed Terms

The draft extends the maximum installment period to 24 months, up from 12 months under v2, so customers can spread the cost of premium products over a longer period.

## Next Steps

- Finance to complete a credit risk assessment with partner banks.
- Sales and CS to review the customer impact.
- The final proposal will be submitted for management approval before any announcement.
