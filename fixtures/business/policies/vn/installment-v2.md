## Effective Date and Scope

This version 2 policy takes effect on March 10, 2026 and supersedes v1 (policies/vn/installment-v1). It applies to all Hikari Home orders in Vietnam paid through installment plans.

## Eligibility

Customers may choose installment payment when the order value is at least 2,000,000 VND. This is lower than the 5,000,000 VND threshold used in v1 and opens installment plans to more mid-priced appliances. Orders below the minimum must be paid in full.

## Installment Terms

The maximum installment period is 12 months, shortened from 24 months under v1 to reduce credit risk. Plans are arranged through partner banks and consumer finance companies, which set the monthly amount according to their own schedules.

## Process

- Sales staff confirm the order value and offer the available plans.
- The partner completes the credit check and approval.
- Finance reconciles partner payments monthly and reports exceptions to the Finance manager.
