## Status

This version 1 policy took effect on July 3, 2025. It has been superseded by v2 (policies/vn/installment-v2) and is retained for reference only. It must not be applied to new orders.

## Eligibility

Under v1, customers in Vietnam could pay for Hikari Home products in installments when the order value was at least 5,000,000 VND. Orders below this threshold had to be paid in full at the time of purchase.

## Installment Terms

The maximum installment period was 24 months. Installments were arranged through partner banks and consumer finance companies, and the monthly amount was calculated by the partner according to its own rate schedule.

## Handling of Existing Agreements

- Installment agreements signed while v1 was in force continue under their original terms.
- Finance staff should refer to v2 when advising new customers or approving new applications.
- Questions about legacy contracts should be sent to the Finance department.
