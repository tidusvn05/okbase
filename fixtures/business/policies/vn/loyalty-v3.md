## Draft status

This document is a draft proposal pending approval. If approved, it would take effect on 1 November 2026 and supersede Loyalty Program Policy (Vietnam) v2 (policies/vn/loyalty-v2). Until approval is confirmed, v2 remains the policy in force.

## Proposed change

The draft proposes extending the points expiry period from 6 months to 12 months after issue. Customer feedback since March 2026 showed that the 6-month window was too short for buyers of large appliances, who purchase infrequently and often lost points before their next order.

## Points to assess

- Impact on point liability and the promotional budget.
- Updates to the customer app and expiry reminder messages.
- Training for store staff and the hotline team.

## Next steps

The CS department will present the proposal to marketing and finance leadership. If approved, a communication plan will be issued at least four weeks before the effective date.
