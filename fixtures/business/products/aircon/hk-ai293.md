## Overview

The HK-AI293 is Hikari Home's mid-range inverter air conditioner, designed for bedrooms and small living rooms in both Vietnam and Japan. It was launched in 2022 and has remained a steady seller in the Sales catalogue since then. This document summarizes the key points the Sales team needs when speaking with retailers and end customers.

## Key Specifications

- Model code: HK-AI293
- Maximum power consumption: 1440 watts at full cooling load
- Launch year: 2022
- Compressor: inverter type with variable-speed control
- Features: AI-assisted temperature control, quiet night mode, washable dust filter

The 1440 watts figure is the peak rating. In normal operation, once the room reaches the target temperature, the inverter compressor slows down and real consumption is considerably lower, which is a strong argument for customers concerned about electricity bills.

## Sales Guidance

Position the HK-AI293 as a reliable, energy-conscious choice for households upgrading from fixed-speed units. Because it has been on the market since 2022, spare parts and service knowledge are widely available across our service network. Sales staff should always confirm room size before recommending the model, and refer installation questions to the CS team.
