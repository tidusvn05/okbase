## Overview

The HK-AI648 is an inverter air conditioner from Hikari Home, launched in 2024. It is designed for bedrooms and small living spaces in both Vietnam and Japan, combining quiet operation with efficient cooling and heating.

## Technical Specifications

- Power consumption: 1120 W
- Launch year: 2024
- Inverter compressor with variable-speed control
- Modes: cooling, heating, dehumidifying and eco
- Low-noise indoor unit suitable for night use
