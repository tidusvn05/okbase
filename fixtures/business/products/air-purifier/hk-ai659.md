## Overview

The HK-AI659 is a large-room air purifier in the Hikari Home lineup, sold in both Vietnam and Japan. It launched in 2023 and targets urban households concerned about dust, pollen and fine particulate matter.

## Key Specifications

- Rated power: 1350 W at maximum operation, including the heater-free air circulation fan and ionizer module.
- Filtration: multi-stage system with a pre-filter, activated carbon layer and HEPA filter.
- Suitable room size: living rooms and open-plan spaces of roughly 60 square meters.
- Controls: touch panel, remote control and an auto mode driven by an air quality sensor.
- Noise: quiet sleep mode designed for bedroom use.

## Sales Notes

Sales teams should position the HK-AI659 as the premium option for larger homes. Filter replacement is recommended about every twelve months, depending on air quality, and replacement filters are stocked in both markets. Customers should be reminded that the unit needs clearance around the intake vents for best performance. Warranty terms follow the standard Hikari Home appliance policy for each region, so please check the current policy before quoting coverage.
