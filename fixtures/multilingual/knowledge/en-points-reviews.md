# Earning points by writing product reviews

Hikari Club members earn 20 points for each product review posted in the app after a verified purchase, and 50 points if the review includes at least two photos or a short video. Reviews must be at least 50 characters long and are checked by our team within 3 working days before the points are credited. Only one review per product per order counts. Copied, offensive or off-topic reviews are rejected and earn nothing. Points from reviews follow the same expiry rules as points earned from purchases.
