# Refund processing timeline

Once a returned item passes inspection at our warehouse, the refund is issued to the original payment method. Credit card refunds appear on the statement within 5 to 10 business days depending on the issuing bank. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer within 3 business days after the customer submits account details. Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the item was defective or we sent the wrong product.
