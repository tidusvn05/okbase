# Sick leave for Vietnam offices

Staff in our Vietnam offices who are unwell should inform their manager before 9:00 on the day of absence. Up to two days of sick leave per month can be taken without a medical certificate and are fully paid by the company. Longer absences require a doctor's note or hospital discharge paper, uploaded to the HR system within 5 days of returning; these days are then paid through social insurance at 75% of the insured salary. Sick days never reduce your annual leave balance.
