# International shipping

We ship to Japan, Korea, Singapore, Thailand and the United States. International orders are dispatched within 2 business days and usually arrive in 7 to 14 days. Import duties and taxes are calculated at checkout and paid by the recipient. Large appliances such as refrigerators cannot be shipped overseas because of voltage and plug differences. Every international parcel includes a tracking number from our courier partner.
