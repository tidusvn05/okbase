# Smart appliance shows Offline in the app

If an appliance that was set up earlier now appears as Offline in the Hikari app, first check that your router is working and the appliance is within range; thick concrete walls can block the signal. Power-cycle the appliance by unplugging it for 30 seconds. Many offline cases are caused by routers that merge the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands under one network name, so turn off band steering or create a separate 2.4 GHz network. If the status is still Offline after 10 minutes, remove the device and add it again.
