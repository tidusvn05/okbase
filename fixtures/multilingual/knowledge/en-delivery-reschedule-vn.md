# Rescheduling a delivery in Vietnam

If you will not be at home, you can reschedule a delivery in Vietnam from the order page in the app until 6 pm the day before. The courier makes up to three delivery attempts and phones you before each one. After three failed attempts the parcel goes back to our warehouse and the order is cancelled; prepaid orders are refunded minus the outbound shipping fee. You can also ask the courier to leave the package with your building security, provided you confirm this in writing by SMS.
