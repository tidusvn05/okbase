# Signing in with Google, Apple or Zalo

You can sign in to the Hikari Home app with a Google, Apple or Zalo account instead of a password. If you already have an account registered with your phone number, link the social login under Settings > Linked accounts so that your orders and points stay in one place; signing in with a new social account creates a separate, empty profile, and accounts cannot be merged later. To unlink a provider, you must first set a password so you do not lose access. LINE login is available only in the Japan app.
