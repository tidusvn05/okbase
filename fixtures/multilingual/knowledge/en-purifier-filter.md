# Replacing the air purifier filter

The HEPA filter in Hikari air purifiers should be replaced every 12 months, or sooner if you live near a busy road. The activated carbon pre-filter can be vacuumed monthly and should be replaced every 6 months. When the filter indicator turns red, open the rear cover, pull the old filter out by its tab and insert the new one with the printed arrow facing the fan. Then hold the Filter Reset button for 5 seconds until the light goes off. Rinsing a HEPA filter with water destroys it, so never wash it.
