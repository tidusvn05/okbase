# Refrigerator error codes

Hikari refrigerators show a code on the door display when something is wrong. E1 means the freezer sensor has failed; food will stay cold for a while, but you should book a repair. E2 is a defrost heater fault, often noticed as ice building up on the back wall. E3 means a door has been left open for more than 3 minutes. E4 indicates that the ice maker water tank is empty or not seated properly. E6 is a compressor start failure: unplug for 10 minutes, then plug back in. Hold the Lock button for 3 seconds to clear the display.
