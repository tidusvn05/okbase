# Typhoon and flood days

When a typhoon or severe flooding is forecast in Vietnam, HR announces a decision by 18:00 the day before. Office staff switch to working from home, and showrooms in the affected cities may close early or stay closed; showroom staff receive full pay for closed days. Never travel to work through flooded streets. Customer deliveries in the affected area are postponed automatically, and customers are notified by SMS. After the storm, managers confirm by message that every team member is safe before business resumes.
