# Changing the phone number on your account

To change the phone number linked to your Hikari Home account, go to Profile > Account details > Phone number. We send a one-time code to both the old and the new number, and you enter both to confirm. If you no longer have access to the old number, visit any showroom with your ID card or upload a photo of your ID in the app, and support will verify you within 2 working days. For security, the number can be changed only once every 30 days, and the new number must not already be registered to another account.
