# Birthday rewards for Hikari Club members

During your birthday month, Hikari Club members in Vietnam receive a voucher in the app: 100,000 VND for Silver, 300,000 VND for Gold and 500,000 VND for Diamond members. The voucher can be used on any order over 1 million VND and expires at the end of that month. You also earn double points on every purchase during your birthday month. Make sure your date of birth is filled in your profile at least 30 days beforehand; the date cannot be changed once saved.
