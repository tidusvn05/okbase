# New employee onboarding

On the first day, new hires receive a laptop, an access badge and accounts for email, chat and the HR system. During the first week they complete mandatory training on information security and the code of conduct. Each newcomer is paired with a buddy from another team for the first month. Managers hold a 30-day check-in meeting to review goals and answer questions.
