# Remote work policy

Employees may work from home up to three days per week after completing their probation period. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, during which staff must be reachable on chat. Working from abroad for more than 14 consecutive days requires written approval from HR because of tax and insurance rules. The company provides a one-time home office allowance of 5 million VND for equipment.
