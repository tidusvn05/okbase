# Refrigerator warranty

Every Hikari refrigerator comes with a 24-month warranty covering parts and labour, and the inverter compressor is covered for 10 years. Because refrigerators are too heavy to carry in, a technician repairs the unit at your home free of charge within the warranty period. Door gaskets, shelves and light bulbs are treated as consumables and are covered for 6 months only. If the fridge stops cooling and cannot be fixed within 72 hours, we reimburse up to 500,000 VND for spoiled food when you show a purchase receipt.
