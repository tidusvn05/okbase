# Travel expense reimbursement

Employees traveling for work can claim reimbursement for transport, hotel and meals. Hotel costs are capped at 1.5 million VND per night domestically and 150 USD per night abroad. Submit claims with original receipts in the expense system within 15 days after returning. Alcohol and personal entertainment are not reimbursable. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.
