# Cancelling a pre-order

New models can be pre-ordered with a 10% deposit before their launch date. You may cancel a pre-order and get the full deposit back at any time until we send the 'ready to ship' notification. After that, cancelling means the deposit is forfeited, unless the launch was delayed by more than 30 days from the date shown when you ordered. Pre-order prices are locked in, and if the official price drops at launch, we refund the difference automatically. Pre-orders cannot be combined with trade-in offers.
