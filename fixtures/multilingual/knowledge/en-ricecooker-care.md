# Caring for the rice cooker inner pot

To make the non-stick coating of your Hikari rice cooker's inner pot last, use only the plastic rice paddle and measuring cup supplied. Wash the pot with a soft sponge and mild detergent after each use; do not rinse rice in it with a metal whisk, and never put the pot in a dishwasher. Wipe the inner lid and steam vent every week, because starch build-up can make the cooker boil over. Replacement inner pots can be ordered from our service centers for about 900,000 VND.
