# Bulk orders for businesses

Companies purchasing 20 units or more of the same model qualify for bulk pricing with discounts from 8% to 15%. Bulk orders include a dedicated account manager, extended 36-month warranty and installation scheduling across multiple sites. Payment terms of net 30 days are available after a credit check. Quotes are valid for 14 days.
