# Security incident response playbook

If you suspect a security incident such as a leaked password, phishing email or unauthorized access, report it immediately in the #security-incident channel and page the on-call engineer. Do not delete evidence or reboot affected machines. The incident commander classifies severity from SEV1 to SEV4 within 30 minutes. For SEV1 and SEV2, affected credentials are rotated right away and customers are notified within 72 hours if personal data was exposed.
