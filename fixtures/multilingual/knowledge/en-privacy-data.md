# Personal data deletion requests

Customers can ask us to delete their personal data at any time by sending a request from the app settings or emailing privacy@hikari.example. We verify identity and complete the deletion within 30 days. Order and invoice records required by tax law are kept for the legal retention period but are no longer used for marketing. Deleting data also closes the customer's account and forfeits remaining loyalty points.
