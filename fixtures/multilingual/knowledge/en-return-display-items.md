# Returns on display and open-box items

Showroom display units and open-box products are sold at up to 40% off and carry an orange 'Trưng bày' sticker. These items cannot be returned simply because you changed your mind. If a hidden defect appears, you may exchange the item within 7 days of purchase as long as the same model is still in stock; otherwise we repair it under warranty. Display units come with a reduced 12-month warranty regardless of product category. Please inspect the item with our staff before paying, because cosmetic scratches are not grounds for an exchange.
