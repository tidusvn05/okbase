# Cash on delivery in Vietnam

Cash on delivery (COD) is available for orders in Vietnam of up to 20 million VND. Please prepare the exact amount, as couriers may not carry change for large notes; you can also pay the courier by scanning a VietQR code. Orders above 20 million VND and pre-orders require online payment in advance. You may open the box and check the product's appearance before paying, but electrical appliances cannot be tested on the doorstep. Accounts that refuse COD parcels three times lose access to cash on delivery.
