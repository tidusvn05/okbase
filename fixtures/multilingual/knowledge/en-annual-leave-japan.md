# Annual paid leave in Japan

Employees at the Tokyo and Osaka offices receive 10 days of paid annual leave once they have worked for six months, rising by one or two days each year up to a maximum of 20 days. Unused days carry over for one year and then expire. By law, everyone entitled to 10 or more days must take at least 5 of them within the year, and HR will assign dates if you fall short. Half-day leave can be used up to 10 times a year. Apply in the attendance system at least one week in advance.
