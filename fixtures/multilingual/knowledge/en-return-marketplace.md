# Returns for orders placed on Shopee, Lazada and Tiki

If you bought a Hikari product from our official store on Shopee, Lazada or Tiki, the return must be requested through that marketplace, not through the Hikari app or a showroom. Each platform applies its own return window, usually 7 days from delivery, and the refund is paid by the platform to your marketplace wallet or original payment method. Our showroom staff cannot accept these items or issue refunds for them. Warranty service, however, is exactly the same as for products bought directly: just bring the serial number to any service center.
