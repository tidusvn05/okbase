# Repair service center opening hours

Our repair service centers in Hanoi (Cau Giay), Da Nang and Ho Chi Minh City (Tan Binh) are open from 8:00 to 17:30, Monday to Saturday, and closed on Sundays and public holidays. The centers handle walk-in repairs for small appliances such as rice cookers, fans and air purifiers; for large appliances, please book a home visit in the app instead. Walk-in customers are served in order of arrival, and diagnosis is free even if you decide not to go ahead with the repair. Items not collected within 90 days after repair are disposed of.
