# Correcting a VAT e-invoice

If your Vietnamese VAT e-invoice contains a mistake such as a wrong tax code, company name or address, contact us within 5 days of receiving it. When only the buyer's name or address is wrong, we issue an adjustment invoice; if the tax code, amount or tax rate is wrong, the original is cancelled and a replacement invoice is issued. Both parties must sign the correction record electronically before the new invoice is sent. Invoices already declared in a previous tax period can only be adjusted, not replaced.
