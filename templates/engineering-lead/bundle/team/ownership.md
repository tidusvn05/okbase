# Engineering ownership map

> Fictional example: replace this record with your own information before relying on it.

## Accountability
The api-team owns issue writes, worker correctness and API contracts. The platform-team owns deployment, infrastructure inventory and monitoring pipelines.
The engineering lead resolves cross-team ownership gaps; ownership does not grant permission to operate a production system.
