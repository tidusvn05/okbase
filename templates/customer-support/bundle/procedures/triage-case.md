# Triage a support case

> Fictional example: replace this record with your own information before relying on it.

## Intake
Record the customer-provided symptom, affected product behavior, event time and source reference. Redact secrets and unrelated personal data.
## Ownership and response
Assign a support owner, link the relevant [product reference](../products/atlas.md) and [FAQ](../faq/notifications.md), and identify missing evidence.
Use the [escalation policy](../policies/escalation.md) if engineering investigation is needed. Draft the customer reply with verified facts and the agreed next update; external sending requires an authorized communication tool.
