# Investigate a missing notification

> Fictional example: replace this record with your own information before relying on it.

## Initial checks
Confirm the issue ID, assignment event time and delivery channel. Ask whether the notification is delayed, missing or duplicated; these are different symptoms.
Check whether the event was accepted and whether delivery evidence is available through an authorized support tool. This starter bundle does not connect to a live customer system.
## Escalate
A symptom alone does not prove data loss. Read [the escalation policy](../policies/escalation.md) and [sample case](../cases/cs-17.md). Record what is known and what engineering must investigate.
