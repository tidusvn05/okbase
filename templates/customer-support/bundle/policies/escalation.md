# Customer issue escalation policy

> Fictional example: replace this record with your own information before relying on it.

## Escalation rule
Escalate a reproducible missing-notification case to the api-team with the issue ID, event time and redacted delivery evidence.
The support owner retains the customer communication record. Engineering owns technical diagnosis; do not claim a root cause before it is verified.
## Promise boundary
State the next update owner and agreed update time. Do not invent a fix date or promise a release outside [the product reference](../products/atlas.md).
Follow [the triage procedure](../procedures/triage-case.md).
