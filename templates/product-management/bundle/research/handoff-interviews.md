## Fictional study
Three fictional team leads described missed handoffs when issue ownership was unclear.
Two described duplicate notifications as distracting; one asked for digest emails. These examples demonstrate evidence capture and are not market validation.
## Interpretation
The sample supports exploring clearer ownership and reliable notifications, but it does not establish willingness to pay or demand across the market.
The [PRD](../requirements/notification-reliability.md) links the selected problem to acceptance criteria; the [scope decision](../decisions/defer-digest.md) records what was deferred.
