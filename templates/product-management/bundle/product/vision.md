# Atlas product vision

> Fictional example: replace this record with your own information before relying on it.

## Target user and outcome
Atlas serves small engineering teams that lose track of issue ownership during handoffs.
The product outcome is fewer unowned issues after a handoff, not simply more notification messages.
The current milestone focuses on reliable notifications. Read the [research findings](../research/handoff-interviews.md), [PRD](../requirements/notification-reliability.md), and [roadmap](../planning/roadmap.md) before proposing additional features.
