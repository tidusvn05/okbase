# Preserve the M1 reliability scope

> Fictional example: replace this record with your own information before relying on it.

## Accepted decision
Keep digest emails outside M1 until notification reliability is measurable.
The rationale is to finish one defensible customer outcome before expanding delivery modes. Revisit after the operating team supplies the reporting evidence and the product owner reviews customer demand.
This decision constrains scope; it does not authorize spending, external communication or deployment.
