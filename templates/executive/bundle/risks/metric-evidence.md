# Reliability reporting evidence gap

> Fictional example: replace this record with your own information before relying on it.

## Risk and owner
The reliability KPI cannot be reported because the source export and reporting query have not been validated.
The platform-team owns providing the export; the api-team owns reviewing the event semantics. The CEO's decision is whether to preserve the acceptance gate while this evidence is missing.
## Mitigation
Request the actual source and review date through authorized tools. Do not replace an unknown KPI value with a synthetic demonstration value.
