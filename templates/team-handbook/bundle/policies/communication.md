# Communication and decision policy

> Fictional example: replace this record with your own information before relying on it.

## Working agreement
A chat discussion becomes an accepted decision only when its owner records the outcome and rationale in the knowledge base.
Use a meeting record for context, a Task for work and a Decision for an approved choice. Link records instead of copying conflicting versions.
## Requests and urgency
For a blocked request, state the owner, missing input and impact. A message marked urgent does not itself grant production access.
See [the request procedure](../procedures/request-help.md) and [ownership](../team/ownership.md).
